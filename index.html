The Story

Just two beekeepers
who couldn’t stop.

BeeWise started in 2019 with one swarm in a Kalk Bay garage and a stubborn refusal to call the exterminator. Six years later we’re still picking up the phone at 5am, still chasing wild swarms up ladders, and still convinced that bees deserve a second chance — every single time.

Every jar we sell, every hive we move, every orchard we visit is part of the same thing: more bees, doing more bee stuff, in more places.

— Thandi & Jan, beekeepers in chief
